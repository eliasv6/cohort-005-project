# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses, work through lessons, and can ask for their money back.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to have the money for one purchase given back, together with their reason. It is Pending until reviewed, then Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, refund (when no money has moved yet)

**Refund**:
Money given back to a learner for a purchase, which exists only once a Refund Request is approved. An approved Refund ends the learner's enrollment in that course.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation

**Refund Eligibility**:
The single set of conditions a purchase must meet before its learner may make a Refund Request: inside the refund window, not too much of the course watched or completed, not a claimed team seat, and no Refund Request already Pending.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a purchase during which a Refund Request may be made.
_Avoid_: Cancel window
